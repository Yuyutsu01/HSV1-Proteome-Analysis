PHASE 11 SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION NOTES
============================================================

1. Intrinsic Cluster Geometry (Internal Validation)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Physicochemical representation exhibited varying silhouette scores across K=2..10,
  with maximum silhouette observed at lower K values.
- ProtBERT representation exhibited distinct cluster density profiles with lower geometric
  dispersion compared to classical descriptors.
- Combined equal-block-weighting balanced the aggregate variance contributions between
  the 25 physicochemical features and 1024 ProtBERT dimensions.

2. Biological Concordance (External Validation)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Adjusted Rand Index (ARI) and Normalized Mutual Information (NMI) measured the degree
  of concordance between unsupervised cluster assignments and independent temporal classes (IE=5, E=15, L=54).
- Because Late proteins constitute 72.97% of the proteome, high cluster purity frequently reflects
  majority-class dominance rather than complete three-class partitioning.
- Permutation testing (1,000 iterations) confirmed that observed agreement levels must be evaluated
  relative to the label-randomized null distribution.

3. Cluster Stability Analysis
------------------------------------------------------------
- Multi-seed K-Means stability across 5 random seeds demonstrated high reproducibility for well-separated
  partitions, with variation observed in boundary proteins.
- Hierarchical clustering (Ward, Average, Complete) provided deterministic cluster trees.

4. Secondary PCA-Reduced Sensitivity
------------------------------------------------------------
- Clustering performed on >=95% variance PCA representations (16 PCs for Phys, 19 for ProtBERT,
  37 for Combined) confirmed consistent relative geometric trends between full and reduced spaces.

5. Predefined Neutrality Rules
------------------------------------------------------------
- No representation is declared 'superior' or 'best'.
- Unsupervised clustering results reflect intrinsic sequence geometry and do not constitute
  mechanistic proof of temporal transcriptional regulation.
