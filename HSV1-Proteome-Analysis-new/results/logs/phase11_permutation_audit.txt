2. Empirical P-Value Formulations & Finite-Sample Correction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Original Formula:
    p_empirical = sum(I(T_null >= T_obs)) / B

- Corrected Standard Finite-Sample Formula (Davison & Hinkley 1997; North et al. 2002):
    p_empirical = (sum(I(T_null >= T_obs)) + 1) / (B + 1)

- Statistical Rationale:
    Under a Monte Carlo permutation test with B randomly sampled permutations from the permutation
    sample space, the observed test statistic represents one valid realization under the null hypothesis.
    The unbiased estimator including the observed data point ensures that p-values are bounded in (0, 1]
    and cannot be strictly zero for finite B (minimum p-value for B=1,000 is 1/1001 ≈ 0.000999001).
